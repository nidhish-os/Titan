Real-time particle field

Particle Storm

A particle sphere that lives on your webcam feed. Move your hand to spin it, pinch to charge it up, and let go to blow it apart. Open your hand to expand the field, make a fist to pull it in tight. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is recorded or uploaded.

Camera mode needs a webcam and a browser that supports getUserMedia. No camera? Mouse and touch give you the same interactions.
